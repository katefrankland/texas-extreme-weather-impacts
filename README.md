## About 

Texas experienced severe winter storms in February 2021, causing a loss of power infrastructure and impacting homes in the Houston area. Being on an independent grid, Texas is unable to import energy from neighboring states at large scales when their power infrastructure goes down. For this assignment, Visible Infrared Imaging Radiometer Suite (VIIRS) data is used, which contains measurements of nighttime brightness, looking at the dates February 7, 2021, and February 16, 2021.
